Move ordering (what gets searched first)
Captures first, most valuable victim taken by the least valuable attacker. Good ordering is why alpha-beta cuts so much.

Each board is one UInt64. Bit 0 is a1 (bottom left), bit 63 is h8 (top right). Hex shown below each.
